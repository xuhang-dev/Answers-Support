A little help

Make yourself at home.

A quick guide to Answers: A Quiet Moment, and a way to reach us whenever you need a hand.

Your first answer

  1. Close the book.

    On iPhone, tap Close the book or swipe right. If the cover is already showing, you are ready.

  2. Hold a question.

    Keep it in your mind. Leave the book closed for at least two uninterrupted seconds, with Answers on screen.

  3. Open gently.

    Tap Open the book or swipe left. A new answer appears. Read it at your own pace.

On iPhone Duo: close and reopen the phone to follow the same ritual. If you launch Answers while the phone is open for the first time, a short guide will invite you to close it first.

Common questions

Why did I get the same answer when I reopened the book?

A quick close and reopen keeps your current answer. To reveal another, keep the book closed for at least two continuous seconds. Several quick closes do not add up to a longer pause.

Does time outside the app count?

No. Keep Answers on screen while the book is closed. Switching to another app or putting Answers in the background interrupts the pause. When you return, close the book and wait two seconds again.

Do I need to enter my question?

No. Simply hold it in your mind. Answers does not record, analyze, or send your question anywhere. It chooses a reflection at random from 284 English answers stored in the app.

Are the answers predictions or personalized advice?

They are prompts for reflection, selected at random. The app does not know your question or circumstances. Take what feels useful and leave room for your own judgment.

What if a longer answer does not fit on screen?

Scroll vertically to read the rest. Answers also follows your iPhone’s text-size settings. Horizontal swipes open or close the book; vertical scrolling lets you stay on the same page.

Will my last answer still be there next time?

Your last answer is saved on this device. On a regular iPhone, the app starts on the cover; opening it quickly shows the saved answer. A full two-second pause reveals a new one. Rotating the screen alone does not change the answer.

Do I need an internet connection or an account?

No. The app works offline, without an account, ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. Your question is never typed into the app. See the privacy policy for details about local storage and support emails.

How can I remove the saved answer?

Deleting the app removes its data from that device. Offloading the app may keep its data, and restoring a device backup may restore it. Answers has no account or cloud-sync service of its own.
