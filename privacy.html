Answers: A Quiet Moment

Privacy policy

Effective October 21, 2026
Published by Xuhang Yi

Your question stays with you. Answers works offline. The app does not collect personal data or send your questions, answers, or app activity to us.

Using the app

This policy covers the English iPhone app Answers: A Quiet Moment, displayed as Answers on your phone, and this support website.

You hold your question in your mind; there is no question-entry field. The app chooses an answer on your device from a built-in collection. It does not make network requests or use a remote AI service.

There are no accounts, advertisements, analytics SDKs, advertising identifiers, or tracking technologies in the app. We do not receive your questions, selected answers, or usage history from the app.

What stays on your device

Answers saves a reference to your last selected answer in local app storage (UserDefaults), so the answer can be restored when you return. It does not save a question, because you never enter one.

Deleting the app removes this local app data from the device. Offloading an app may preserve its data. Your operating system’s backup and restore features, including iCloud device backups if enabled, may include or restore app data; Answers does not operate its own cloud-sync service.

If you email support

Contacting xuhang.dev@gmail.com is optional. When you do, we receive your email address and the contents of your message, including any attachments or device details you choose to provide.

We use this information to respond to your request and investigate the issue you report. Your email service and our email provider process the message as part of delivering and storing email. Please do not include sensitive information that is not needed for support.

We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to respond to and resolve your inquiry, or to meet applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion of your support correspondence by emailing xuhang.dev@gmail.com. If information must be retained to meet a legal obligation, we will explain that when responding to your request.

Visiting this website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added analytics scripts, advertising, external fonts, or tracking cookies to these pages.

The hosting service still processes the standard information needed to serve web requests. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. Its handling of hosting and website information is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement and GitHub Pages data-collection documentation.

Following an external link or opening your email app takes you to a separate service with its own privacy practices.

Changes and questions

If the app’s data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date.

For questions about this policy, contact Xuhang Yi at xuhang.dev@gmail.com. For help using the app, visit Support.
